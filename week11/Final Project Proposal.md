# Final Project Proposal

For my final project, I want to create an interactive journey visualization based on my own experiences commuting in New York City and traveling to different cities. I often use Google Maps to plan my trips, but knowing how to get somewhere does not always mean knowing what problems I might encounter along the way. For example, a broken elevator can be a problem when traveling with luggage, and it can create an even bigger barrier for people with disabilities.

I plan to combine Transitland routing data, NYC 311 unresolved traffic and pedestrian signal reports, Accessible Pedestrian Signal (APS) locations, and MTA elevator outage data. Users will be able to enter their starting point and destination, visualize their journey, and compare potential barriers along different routes.

Through this project, I want to explore how public data and interactive visualization can help people better prepare for their journeys and make everyday travel more accessible.
