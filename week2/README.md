# Week 2 notes

This idea came to me after class. That day, I went to exercise at a park near my home and saw an absurd number of pigeons, so I suddenly thought, let's make a pigeon for this assignment! My process was actually kind of backwards. I first drew the final complete pigeon, and then started breaking it down step by step. V5 is the complete pigeon, and V4 turns it into a flying pigeon. After that, I was thinking maybe I should just draw some random shapes, so that became V1. The more I looked at those shapes, the more they reminded me of things floating in space, so V2 became an orbit with lots of shapes floating on it, and V3 became something like a gravity field that pulls the shapes together. When I translated the paper versions into code, I made a few changes. The colors in V1 became much brighter. In V2, I added random() so the shapes on the orbit can change color, size, position, and line weight. In V3, the objects are also randomized, but within a smaller range, and their positions are much more concentrated. In V4, I changed the position, angle, and size so the pigeon looks like it is flying from the bottom left to the upper right. V5 stayed pretty close to my original paper version. I thought the paper versions looked kind of ugly (cuz I am so bad at drawing) and were hard to adjust, but once I coded them, I actually thought they looked pretty cute. It was also much easier to quickly change the position, size, angle, and color. I think it is because with code, I can control almost every detail just by changing the numbers.
