Week 8

Webcam Interaction

For this project, I just wanted to try something fun with the webcam and see what I could do with the pixels.

I tried two different ideas:

  • Warm Color:Particle Mode: I wanted to see what it would look like if the camera image was made of moving circles. I used the RGB values to change their size and movement.
  • Cold Color:Light Drawing Mode: I thought it would be fun to draw with a flashlight, so I made the camera follow the brightest point and leave blue trails behind.

I also wanted to try using colors to control the modes instead of just pressing different keys. So when I press S, the camera checks the colors and decides which mode to use.

Controls

  • S – Scan colors and switch modes
  • C – Clear the screen (at light drawing mode)
